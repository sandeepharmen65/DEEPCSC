1. Privacy policy

Who operates this service: Deep CSC, Chhajli, Sunam, Punjab. For privacy questions or requests, contact Deep CSC on WhatsApp at +91 94787 27562.

Information customers may provide

  • Name and mobile number; an email address is optional on application forms.
  • Selected service, application or certificate reference, booking address, landmark, preferred visit time, and distance.
  • Files the customer chooses to attach, such as identity proofs, certificates, job notices, photos or signatures.
  • Payment transaction information handled by the payment provider when a live approved checkout is enabled. Deep CSC does not ask customers to send card PINs, CVVs, passwords or OTPs in a message.

Why information is used

To understand the requested service, prepare a checklist, review customer-submitted documents, confirm the government and service fees, respond to enquiries, arrange an approved home visit, and address complaints or payment questions. Information should only be used for the service the customer requested and any legally required recordkeeping.

Sharing and storage

If the customer gives the displayed consent and submits a service request while the backend is connected, Deep CSC's request system stores the customer's name, mobile number, optional email, selected service, written notes, selected document filenames, request status and timestamps so the owner can follow up. Document contents are not uploaded to or stored by this website or its backend. The customer shares the actual JPG/PDF files only through the app they choose, such as WhatsApp, which may process or retain them under its own policy. Request information is retained for handling the request and applicable recordkeeping; contact Deep CSC to ask about correction or deletion. A future secure upload feature would need its own storage, access controls and deletion schedule before collecting file contents.

If the customer approves a final amount and chooses to pay, an enabled hosted payment checkout may receive the required payment and order data. Government portals receive documents only when an application is actually submitted with the customer's authorization. No application result, certificate or government approval is guaranteed.

Choice and requests

Customers can choose not to send a request or file. They may contact Deep CSC to ask for correction or deletion of information held in Deep CSC's control; legal retention duties and information already held by WhatsApp, a payment provider or a government portal may limit deletion. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules have phased commencement; this policy does not claim that every provision is already in force.

Children and representatives

Where a service concerns a child or another person, the customer must have the authority required to submit that person's information. A parent or lawful guardian should make the request when required by law or by the relevant portal.

3. Cookie & local-storage policy

The current customer site does not use advertising pixels or analytics trackers. It uses browser local storage to remember saved service favourites and the customer's cookie choice. These entries stay in that browser profile and are not a customer account or secure document store.

Optional Google Fonts are requested only if the customer chooses “Accept optional” on the cookie banner. If rejected, the site uses device fonts. The cookie banner choice is stored locally so the banner does not repeat on each visit. The customer can change the choice using “Cookie choices” in the footer or clear browser storage.

WhatsApp opens only after the customer clicks a WhatsApp link or submits a consented request. Razorpay's hosted checkout script is requested only after an approved quote is loaded and the customer gives payment-specific consent. These providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies after the customer leaves or interacts with their service; consult their policies.

4. Refund & cancellation policy

Deep CSC will show the final amount and work description before accepting payment. Customers should contact Deep CSC on WhatsApp as soon as they want to cancel or correct a request.

  • Before work starts: a cancellation and refund request will be reviewed promptly. Any deduction will be made only where permitted by law and disclosed in the approved quote.
  • After work has started: Deep CSC will review the work completed and any government-portal or payment-provider charge already incurred. Any refundable balance will be assessed fairly and under applicable law; the portal's own refund rules may apply to amounts paid to government authorities.
  • Duplicate or failed payment: contact Deep CSC with the transaction reference. A verified duplicate or reversed payment will be handled through the original payment channel where available.
  • Service error or non-delivery: Deep CSC will investigate and offer an appropriate correction or refund where required by law and the circumstances.

Refunds approved by Deep CSC will be initiated through the original payment method, subject to payment-provider processing times and applicable requirements. This policy does not remove any consumer remedy available under law.

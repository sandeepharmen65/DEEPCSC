  • Duplicate or failed payment: contact Punjab CSC with the transaction reference. A verified duplicate or reversed payment will be handled through the original payment channel where available.
  • Service error or non-delivery: Punjab CSC will investigate and offer an appropriate correction or refund where required by law and the circumstances.
